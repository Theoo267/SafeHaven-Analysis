# Capstone Project Proposal – Comparison of the Behavior of Safe-Haven Assets

→ **This document reflects the initial project proposal. During the course of the project, the research question and methodological approach were adjusted and expanded to better align with the objectives.**

Project title: Comparison of the behavior of safe-haven assets in response to financial market volatility.

The objective of this project is to analyze and compare the performance of several traditional and alternative 
safe-haven assets in periods of financial instability. During times of market turbulence, investors tend 
to seek assets that can preserve value and limit risk exposure. Historically, assets such as gold, 
government bonds (T-Bills), and the U.S. dollar have been considered reliable safe havens. However, 
the emergence of digital assets like Bitcoin has challenged this traditional view by introducing a 
decentralized and highly volatile alternative.
This study will examine how these different assets behave during periods of high volatility, as measured 
by the VIX index and major stock market indicators such as the S&P 500. Using historical data retrieved 
from Yahoo Finance and other reliable sources, the project will compute returns, volatility measures, and 
correlation coefficients to assess the degree of protection offered by each asset.
The main goal is to determine which assets best fulfill the function of a safe haven in times of market 
stress and to evaluate whether traditional assets continue to outperform newer or alternative ones in 
stabilizing investment portfolios.

For the data science component, I plan to combine two analyses:

- A linear regression model to evaluate how financial market volatility (VIX) and market performance (S&P 500) influence the returns of traditional safe-haven assets such as gold, U.S. Treasuries, and the U.S. dollar.

- A K-Means clustering analysis to identify different market regimes (stable, volatile, crisis) and analyze how these assets behave under each regime.

This combination provides both a predictive and exploratory perspective.

My research question would be:

To what extent do safe-haven assets such as gold, U.S. Treasuries, and the U.S. dollar respond to changes in market volatility (VIX), and how does their behavior differ across market regimes identified through data science methods?
